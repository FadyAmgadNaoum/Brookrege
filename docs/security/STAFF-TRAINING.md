# Security training for Brookrege staff

A 45-minute session for everyone with an admin account, at onboarding and once a year. The trainer goes through
each section, shows it live in the admin, and finishes with the quiz. Keep the signed attendance list at the bottom.

## 1. Your account (10 min)

- **Password**: at least 12 characters, not used anywhere else. A password manager (Bitwarden, 1Password, or the browser's) is the easy way. The admin refuses weak and common passwords — including ones with "brookrege" or "sohag" in them.
- **Two-step verification (2FA)**: My account › Two-step verification. Install Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or Authy, scan the QR code. It's optional (a super admin can require it for everyone in Security › Policy), and recommended.
- **Backup codes**: shown once when you turn on 2FA. Print them or save them in the password manager — **not** in a photo on the same phone. Each works once.
- **Lost phone?** Tell a super admin at once; they reset your 2FA and sign you out everywhere.
- **Sessions**: you're signed out after 60 minutes without activity and after 12 hours in any case. My account › Sessions shows where you're signed in; sign out anything you don't recognise.
- **Locked out after 5 wrong attempts?** Wait 15 minutes or ask a super admin to unlock. You'll get an email when your account is locked — if it wasn't you, tell a super admin.

## 2. Phishing and phone scams (10 min)

- Brookrege will **never** ask for your password or a 2FA code by phone, WhatsApp or email — not even the owner or "IT".
- Always open the admin by typing `admin.brookrege.com` or using your bookmark — never from a link in a message.
- Be suspicious of urgency ("the owner needs this now"), unusual payment requests, or a "customer" sending files/links to open.
- Someone phones claiming to be a customer and asks what we have about another person → refuse; only that person, verified, gets their data (§4).

## 3. Devices and data (10 min)

- Screen lock on phones and laptops; don't leave the admin open on a shared computer — sign out.
- Don't copy customers' phone numbers or messages into personal WhatsApp groups, Excel files or notebooks. The admin is where they belong: it deletes them automatically after the retention period; your copies don't.
- Don't install browser extensions you don't need (they can read the admin page).
- Public Wi-Fi is fine (the admin is always encrypted), but lock your laptop.

## 4. Customers' personal data (10 min)

- Use a visitor's details **only** to answer their request. No marketing lists, no passing numbers to developers or other brokers without the customer's agreement.
- Someone asks what we hold about them, for a copy, or to be deleted: tell a super admin. They verify it's really that person (call back the number on file), then use Admin › Privacy. We must answer within 30 days.
- Staff notes: write only what's needed for the sale ("prefers evenings", "budget 2M"), nothing about health, religion, family problems or other sensitive matters.

## 5. When something looks wrong (5 min)

Tell the incident lead (Fady) **immediately** — by phone — if you:
- get a security alert email (password or 2FA changed, account locked, backup code used) you didn't cause, or see activity you didn't do;
- clicked a suspicious link or typed your password somewhere odd;
- lost a phone or laptop with the admin on it;
- see customer data somewhere it shouldn't be.

Reporting fast is what matters; nobody gets blamed for reporting. The full plan is `INCIDENT-RESPONSE.md`.

## Quiz (answers below)

1. A caller says he's from Brookrege IT and needs your 6-digit code to "fix your account". What do you do?
2. Where should you keep your backup codes?
3. A customer emails asking you to delete his details. What do you do?
4. You clicked a link and typed your admin password on a page that looked odd. What now?
5. Can you save a list of this week's leads in an Excel file on your laptop?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Refuse and hang up; tell the incident lead. Nobody legitimate ever asks for the code.
2. Printed and stored safely, or in a password manager — not in a phone photo.
3. Pass it to a super admin, who verifies it's him and erases via Admin › Privacy (within 30 days).
4. Change your password now (My account), then call the incident lead — they'll sign you out everywhere and check the activity log.
5. No — work in the admin. Exports for reports use Analytics & reports and are deleted when no longer needed.
