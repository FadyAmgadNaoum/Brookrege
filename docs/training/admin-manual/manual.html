Roles: who can do what

Every staff member has one of three roles. A super admin sets it in Team. The menu on the left only shows what your role allows.

Publish, renew, take offline, mark as soldYesYesYes

Delete listingsYesYesNo
Regions, compounds & projectsYesYesView only
Inquiries and property submissions (view and update)YesYesInquiries yes; submissions view only

On the Team screen the roles are described as: Super admin — “Everything, including team and activity log”; Content admin — “Listings, photos, compounds, leads — no team or system settings”; Moderator — “View listings, renew or take them offline, handle inquiries — no editing or deleting”.

Keep super admins few. Two people is enough: the owner and one trusted manager. Everyone else works as a content admin or moderator.
Chapter 2 · everyone

Signing in and two-step verification

Signing in

  1. Open https://admin.brookrege.com. You see Sign in to Brookrege admin.
  2. Enter your Email and Password, click Sign in.
  3. If two-step verification is on for you, the page changes to Two-step verification: type the 6-digit code from your authenticator app and click Sign in. No phone? Click Use a backup code and type one of your printed codes (each works once).

First sign-in

A super admin gives you a temporary password in person or by phone. After signing in you must choose your own password before you can do anything else.

Password rules

  • At least 12 characters (up to 128), with a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter, a number and a symbol.
  • No common words (“password”, “admin”, “brookrege”, “sohag”…), no sequences like 12345 or qwert, no character repeated 4 times, and not your name or email.
  • Tip: three unrelated words plus a number and symbol, e.g. Nile-Tea-Window-47! (don't use this one).

Setting up two-step verification

My account › Two-step verification: scan the QR code with Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or similar, type the current code to confirm. You then get 10 backup codes: print them or keep them in a password manager, not in a photo on the same phone. It's optional for every role; a super admin can require it for everyone in Security.

Being signed out

  • After 60 minutes without activity, and in any case 12 hours after signing in, you are signed out and must sign in again.
  • Signing out elsewhere, a role change or a suspended account also signs you out at once.

Locked account

After 5 wrong passwords or codes in a row the account locks for 15 minutes; every further 5 mistakes double the wait (30 min, 1 h … up to 24 h). A super admin can unlock you early in Team or Security › Staff accounts › Unlock.

Never share your password or codes, even with a colleague or “technical support” on the phone. Brookrege staff will never ask for them. Report such a call to the owner.

Chapter 4 · content admins (moderators: status buttons only)

Listings: add, publish, renew, sell

The life of a listing

Draft→ Publish →Live (3 months)→ date passes →Expired→ Renew →Live
Live→ Mark as sold →Sold→ Archive →Archived→ Move back to drafts →Draft

A listing is online for 3 months from publishing or renewing. When the date passes it disappears from the public site immediately and is marked Expired by the nightly job (02:00). Expired listings stay in the admin so you can renew them.

The Listings screen

Tabs: All, Live, Expiring soon (14 days), Drafts, Expired, Sold. Search with Search by title or address. The table shows price, status, expiry date (with “in N days” when close) and the number of leads. “— no photos” warns about listings without pictures.

Adding a listing

Click Add listing. Fields:

FieldRules
Title in ArabicRequired, at least 5 characters. Shown on the Arabic site.
Title in EnglishOptional; the English site shows the Arabic title if empty.
Listed forSale or Rent. Rent is for apartments and shops only.
Property typeApartments, Villas, Townhouses, Studios, Shops, Offices, Land.
SellerSales only, required: Developer or Resale.
Price (EGP)Whole pounds; for rent it is the Monthly rent (EGP).
Area (m²)Required.
Bedrooms / Bathrooms0–50. No bedrooms for shops, offices and land; no bathrooms for land.
Description in Arabic / EnglishOptional but strongly recommended.
Feature this listing…Shows it at the top of results. Use for a few listings at a time.
Region / CompoundRegion required. Compound optional (Not in a compound); only compounds of the chosen region appear.
Address or landmark, Latitude, LongitudeOptional. Coordinates put the listing on the map: in Google Maps right-click the spot and click the numbers to copy them.

Then click Save and publish (goes live for 3 months) or save as a draft to finish later.

Editing and status buttons

Open a listing to change its details (Save changes) and use the Status buttons:

Delete listing removes it from the site and the admin list; the activity log keeps a record. Prefer Archive — it keeps the history and reports complete.

Changes appear on the website within about a minute (the site keeps very short copies of pages to stay fast under heavy traffic).
Chapter 5 · content admins

Photos and video

  • On a listing, drag files onto Drop photos or a video here, or click to choose, or click Add from media library to reuse an existing upload.
  • Accepted: JPG, PNG, WebP up to 15 MB each; MP4, MOV, WebM up to 95 MB; up to 20 files at a time. Other files are refused with a reason.
  • Photos are resized automatically (three sizes, so phones download small ones). Videos show Preparing video… for a few minutes while a preview frame is made.
  • The first photo is the Cover photo; choose another with its cover button. Remove takes a photo off this listing (it stays in the media library).
  • Good photos: daylight, landscape orientation, the best room first, no watermarks of other agencies, no people's faces or car plates.
Chapter 6 · everyone (changes: content admins)

Media library

Every photo and video uploaded. Tabs: All, Photos, Videos, Not used, Deleted. Each file shows where it is used (In 2 listings or Not used).

  • Add description: a short text such as “Living room with balcony”. Helps blind visitors and Google.
  • Delete: if the file is in use you are asked whether to delete it everywhere. Deleted files can be Restored for 30 days.
  • Uploads never attached to a listing are cleared automatically after 7 days.
Chapter 7 · everyone

Inquiries

People who asked to be called about a listing. Each new inquiry is also emailed to the staff list (chapter 12), and the visitor may get an SMS confirmation.

New→Contacted→Viewing booked→Offer made→Closed
  • Change the status with the drop-down on each inquiry; use Spam for fake requests.
  • Write a staff note (“called, wants to visit Thursday”) — it saves when you click away.
  • Call back within one working day. The owner sees the “median time to first response” in Analytics, and monitoring warns when many inquiries wait for more than a day.
Phone numbers and messages are personal data. Don't copy them to personal WhatsApp groups or spreadsheets; after 24 months without activity the system removes the contact details automatically.

Property submissions

Owners who used “Add your property” on the website. Statuses: New → Contacted → Listed or Not suitable. When you list it, click Add listing, create the listing, then set the submission to Listed.

Chapter 9 · content admins

Regions, compounds & projects

  • Regions (Add region): areas of Sohag. Name in English (used in web addresses) and Arabic name.
  • Compounds (Add compound): Name in English / Arabic, region, Developer, descriptions. Each compound gets its own page on the site listing its units and “starting from” prices.
  • Projects (Add project): Partnership / major project or Completed / sold, with optional Partner and Completed on date. Shown on the “Our projects” page.
  • A region or compound that still has listings can't be deleted; move the listings first.
Chapter 10 · content admins and super admins

Analytics & reports

Choose a period (From – to). Tabs: Overview, Listings, Inquiries, Team activity.

  • Figures: Listing views, Inquiries, Inquiries per 100 views, 'Add your property' requests, New listings published, Marked sold, Live listings now, Median time to first response, each compared with the previous period.
  • Charts: Views and inquiries per day, By property type, and the Most viewed listings table.
  • Export to Excel, or Print / save as PDF (the browser's print window; choose “Save as PDF”).
  • Scheduled reports › Add schedule: Weekly (Sundays) or Monthly (the 1st), with recipient emails. Stop ends a schedule. Needs email set up (chapter 12).

Views are counted once per visitor per listing per 30 minutes; search-engine robots are not counted.

Chapter 11 · super admins

Team

  1. Add member: Name, Email, Role, Temporary password.
  2. Give the temporary password in person or by phone — never by email or chat. They choose their own at first sign-in (until then the list shows Hasn't chosen a password yet).
  • Suspend blocks someone at once (e.g. they left the company) and signs them out everywhere. Restore access undoes it.
  • The list shows Last sign-in, Two-step status and Locked accounts.
  • When someone leaves: suspend the same day, then check Security › Signed in now is clear for them.
Chapter 12 · super admins

Notifications (email and SMS)

Emails and text messages are sent automatically in the background and retried if a provider is briefly unavailable. Tabs:

  • Email: provider SendGrid, From address (verified in SendGrid), From name, and Staff who receive new inquiries and submissions. Save email settings, then Send test.
  • SMS: provider Twilio (Account SID, sender or Messaging Service SID). Egypt requires a registered alphanumeric sender ID; Arabic messages use 70 characters per SMS segment. Don't send — write to server log (testing) is for testing only.
  • Messages: the texts of each automatic email/SMS; choose one, edit, Save template.
  • Delivery log: every message with Sent, Failed or Skipped and the error. Background jobs: failed jobs with Retry.

Provider keys are stored encrypted and shown masked; you only type them again to change them.

Chapter 13 · super admins

Security

“Who can get in, how, and what happened recently.” Tabs:

  • Overview: anything that Needs attention (locked accounts, required 2FA not set up) and the Policy switch Require two-step verification for everyone (off = optional for everyone).
  • Staff accounts: status of each person, with Unlock, Reset 2FA (when someone lost their phone and codes — check it is really them first) and Sign out everywhere.
  • Signed in now: every signed-in browser; End session signs one out.
  • Allowed networks: limit the admin to the office connection. Only turn this on if the office has a fixed IP address from its provider — most Egyptian home and mobile connections change address. Add my current address first, so you don't lock yourself out. Saved changes apply on all servers within 15 seconds.
  • Recent events: sign-ins, wrong passwords, locks, 2FA changes, blocked addresses.
Emergency: if you think an account is stolen, use Sign out everywhere for that person, suspend them in Team, and call the technical contact. Full steps: docs/security/INCIDENT-RESPONSE.md.
Chapter 14 · super admins

Privacy requests

Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law gives people the right to know what we hold about them, get a copy, and have it removed.

  1. Check it is really them — e.g. call back the number they gave.
  2. Requests › Find a person's data: enter the Phone (any format) or email, Search. You see their inquiries, property requests and messages.
  3. Download a copy (JSON) and send it through the channel they contacted you from.
  4. To remove: Erase this person's details, type ERASE, confirm. Names, phones, emails, messages and staff notes are removed; the records stay without them so reports still add up. This can't be undone.

Retention: how long personal details are kept (leads: months without any update; message logs and sign-in addresses). A clean-up runs every night at 04:10; Run the clean-up now runs it immediately.

Chapter 15 · everyone

My account

  • Password: change it (current password required).
  • Two-step verification: turn on (chapter 2), Create new backup codes (the old ones stop working; you'll be told when you're running low), or turn off if your role allows.
  • Signed-in browsers: every place you are signed in. Sign out everywhere else after using a shared or lost device.
Chapter 16 · super admins

Activity log

A permanent record of every change: who, what, when (e.g. “Published listing”, “Edited listing”, “Updated inquiry”, “Reset two-step verification”). Filter with Everything, Listings, Inquiries and others; Show changes displays before/after values. Nobody — not even a super admin — can edit or delete entries.

Chapter 18

When something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to do
“Email or password is incorrect.”Check caps lock and the email. After 5 tries the account locks — ask a super admin to unlock.
Lost phone with the authenticatorSign in with a backup code, then set up 2FA again. No codes: a super admin uses Reset 2FA.
Signed out unexpectedlyNormal after 60 minutes idle or 12 hours. If it happens constantly, tell the super admin.
A photo was refusedThe message says why: wrong type (HEIC from iPhones — set the camera to “Most compatible”), too large, or not really an image.
Listing not on the websiteIs it Live and not expired? Wait a minute. Still missing: refresh with Ctrl+F5.
No inquiry emailsNotifications › Delivery log. Failures there: tell the super admin (provider key or credit).
The admin or site is downThe technical team is alerted automatically. If it lasts more than 15 minutes, call the technical contact.
A suspicious call or email asks for a password or codeRefuse, hang up, tell the owner. That's how most accounts are stolen.
